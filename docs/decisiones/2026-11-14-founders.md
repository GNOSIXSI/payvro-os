# Founder decisions · 2026-11-14 (Ulises Arellano and Ricardo Rosenzweig, recorded by the orchestrator)

These override any earlier text in this repo. WP-OS-01 / WP-CL-01 apply them to the docs before writing code.

1. **License (OS): FSL-1.1-ALv2** (Functional Source License, Apache 2.0 future license).
   Any person or business may use, modify and charge its own customers with PAYVRO OS; offering it as a competing
   service is not allowed during the FSL period; each version becomes Apache 2.0 two years after release.
   Add `NOTICE` ("PAYVRO, created by GNOSIX — Ulises Arellano and Ricardo Rosenzweig"; keep it in derivatives) and
   `TRADEMARKS.md` (the PAYVRO name and logos are not licensed). Public wording: "código disponible (fair-code)".
   Not legal advice; a Mexican lawyer reviews before Cloud charges anyone.
   Cloud stays private, all rights reserved.
2. **Order payer page: native CoDi QR by default** (single amount, expiration 24 h) plus CoDi push to the payer's
   phone; the pey payment link is the fallback ("Pagar con link"). A second payment to the same QR goes to
   `en_revision`. Rationale: the jury weighs payer-clear CoDi use; pey links may allow CARD. Supersedes the earlier
   ADR-0006 decision (update ADR 0006 and ARCHITECTURE).
3. **Submission URL:** the open DEMO deployment; the LIVE $0.01 charge is shown as redacted evidence plus video.
4. **`en_revision`:** the owner may release manually with a mandatory written reason (logged), or "pedir la
   diferencia" (new charge for the shortfall), or "no liberar" (optional note "devolución hecha por fuera").
5. **Cloud landing stays private** until M2; on Sunday only PAYVRO OS competes.
6. **Public text:** founders' names in README credits: yes. Attribute pey statements as "per pey (2026-11-07)"
   or omit them. Keep agent lane names in AGENTS/PLAN (they are part of the public build story).
7. **Free plan:** its one template can be any template, including Mostrador. **Membership:** monthly CoDi request via
   a payment link valid 7 days plus a push as quick notice; reminders on day 1 and 3; "Pausada por falta de pago" on
   day 7; the owner configures the days.
8. Validation time copy: "hasta 5 días hábiles" (pey docs).
9. **One frontend for both editions.** The UI is built once, in payvro-os (`app/`, `components/`), from the design
   reference `docs/design/payvro-pantallas-ux.html` (Fable 5.2, 13 screens / 80 states, with a Cloud/OS edition switch).
   Cloud reuses it by the same copy-with-provenance as the engine (`ui.lock.json`, gate fails on drift) and adds only
   Cloud screens: landing, plan & billing, assisted pey signup, business switcher, support console. Edition differences
   go behind one setting (`PAYVRO_EDICION=os|cloud`); never fork a shared screen. The design file is a reference, not
   pixels to copy: builders follow `docs/design/AJUSTES.md` (tweaks list) where it differs.
